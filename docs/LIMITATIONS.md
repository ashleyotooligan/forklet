# Limits worth preserving

## Not an intelligence claim

Forklet is an eight-unit hand-specified recurrent model. It has no learned language model, training dataset, reward optimization, tool planning, biological tissue, consciousness model, quantum circuit, or general intelligence evaluation.

The words “mind” and “future” are narrative framing for a stateful decision system and its simulated continuations. They do not establish sentience or predict actual future events.

## Not a real market

The fictional price path is generated before agents act. There is no order book, AMM, market impact, execution delay, adversary, live feed, gas estimation or wallet. Fee and exposure rules are simple deterministic accounting constraints.

Changing a bulletin can produce a favorable paper balance in one scenario without providing any evidence of profitable real trading. Model parameters are designed to make information effects inspectable, not fitted or validated for markets.

## Not general causal identification

Paired copies isolate the declared intervention within this program under its shared-world and shared-randomness assumptions. This is not an estimate of how a real investor, language model, organism or market would respond. There is no empirical external validation.

## Finite memory and bounded state

The explicit history contains only six observations. Recurrent differences can fade, saturate, persist, or reach identical floating-point values. Equal final states do not imply that trajectories were always equal. Equal actions do not imply equal internal states.

## Reproducibility is scoped

Serializing all state is necessary for replay, but does not prove an implementation is correct. The short ID is not a secure commitment. Floating-point math may vary in the final digits across runtimes. The native rendering pipeline is not a browser compatibility test.

## Honest figures

Figures are generated from the implemented engine, and character art is separately identified as illustrative. Screenshots are not evidence of a deployed chain application or production model. A future live-data or on-chain adapter must be documented as a new component and tested on its own terms.
